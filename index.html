Class 8 Science Lesson Plans

Chapter: Force

Academic Standards

  • Knowledge & Understanding: Understand the concept of force, its effects, and differentiate between contact and non-contact forces.
  • Skills: Develop observation, experimentation, and classification skills.
  • Application: Apply concepts of force to explain daily life phenomena.
  • Attitude & Values: Exhibit curiosity, teamwork, and appreciation for physical laws.

Period 1: Introduction to Force and its Effects (45 Mins)

Teaching StepsTeacher ActivitiesLearner ActivitiesBlack Board WorkTLM
IntroductionShows a ball at rest. Asks a student to push it. Asks another to stop a rolling ball.Observes and tries to push/stop the ball.Football, toy car
Mind MappingDraws a central circle "Force" and asks students to brainstorm words.Shares words like push, pull, motion.Central circle: FORCE -> Push, PullChalk, Mind map chart
Probing QuestionsWhat did you do to move the ball? What did you do to stop it?Answers: Pushed it, applied force to stop it.
Declaration of TopicDeclares: "Today we will learn about Force and its effects."Listens and notes the topic.Topic: Force and its Effects
Reading & KeywordsAsks students to read the first section. Instructs to underline keywords.Reads text and identifies keywords.Textbook
Group DiscussionDivides students into groups to discuss keywords.Discusses keywords like push, pull, interaction.Keywords: Push, Pull, Interaction
DefinitionDefines force: "A push or a pull on an object."Notes the definition in notebooks.Definition: Force is a push or a pull.
Conceptual UnderstandingExplains that force is an interaction between two objects.Listens and asks doubts.
ActivityGives a rubber band and a sponge. Asks to stretch and squeeze.Stretches and squeezes objects.Rubber bands, sponges
ConclusionSummarizes: Force is a push or pull. It can change shape. It requires interaction.Listens and writes summary.
Culminatory ActivityAsks to name 3 actions at home involving push or pull.Opens door (pull), moves chair (push).
EvaluationAsks: 1. Define force. 2. Give example of force changing shape.Answers orally.
Home TaskList 5 activities where force changes shape.Notes down homework.
